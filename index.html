Aquarius Project · Greenland Mission

Surveying Greenland's seamounts & underwater volcanoes

As part of the Aquarius Project, our team deployed a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) off the coast of Greenland to survey submarine seamounts and underwater volcanoes. Each dive traced a transect down the volcanic flanks, logging position and depth at every fix and capturing imagery of the seafloor. This page brings the mission together: a map of the locations we surveyed, an interactive 3D model of the ROV, and a gallery of imagery from the dives.
