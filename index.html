Greenland Mission · ROV Étaín

Aquarius Project — Greenland ROV Survey

Across the mission we surveyed an underwater volcano (the Caldera), an isolated seamount, two glacier-carved valleys, and a fault line. This page brings it all together — a map of every dive we ran, an interactive 3D model of the ROV, and a gallery of imagery from the seafloor.

For this mission the ROV carried a newly integrated instrument skid. Its hydraulic arms collected animals at depth and pushed cores to gather sediment, a Niskin bottle captured water samples, and a temperature sensor supplied by the science team was added to the unit. Two 4K cameras — one facing forward and one facing downward — recorded the seafloor throughout.
